Single gates

Red steady — Stop. Confirmed closed. Do not pass.
Green — Operating. Steady when confirmed open; flashing while the gate is moving.
Amber flashing — Issue. Fault, failed command, offline, no recent heartbeat, or not yet commissioned.

Weather

Safety rule kept: red and green are shown only when a controller has reported that position on a heartbeat less than 90 seconds old. Everything else shows amber. Today every Balwyn gate is uncommissioned, so in the live portal all of them would show amber — Issue until a controller connects. That is correct, not a fault in the display.
