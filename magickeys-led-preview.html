Status indicators — gates, doors and booms

Every state the portal can actually produce, in the order the safety logic checks them.

LightMeansConvention
Confirmed openSteady green — safe to pass
Confirmed closed / secureSteady red — do not pass
Moving, or position not confirmedFlashing amber — keep clear, same as a boom in motion
Fault, or a command that failedFast flashing red — needs attention
No contact / not commissionedDark, slow breath — the portal is not being told anything

The rule this enforces: green and steady red are shown only when a controller has reported that position on a heartbeat less than 90 seconds old. A stale, faulted or silent controller goes dark or amber — it never guesses a position. That is the same conservatism the existing text labels use; the light just keeps “moving” and “fault” visible instead of folding them into “unknown”.
